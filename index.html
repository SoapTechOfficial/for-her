The Little Corner

there was, somewhere in the endless noise of the internet, a little corner that nobody had really asked for.

it wasn't important to anyone else.

it wasnt supposed to be.

it was simply a quiet place, for when hannahs thoughts got to loud, and she needed somewhere to chill.

and every night, when everything outside became a little quieter, someone could come back to it.

there was no reason to rush here.

no one demanding something be done. no list of things to do.

just a little corner, and alot of love.

and maybe that was enough.

because sometimes the nicest things aren't the things that make the most noise.

sometimes they're the things you almost miss.

a text waiting when you weren't expecting one. a memory that suddenly makes you smile. someone remembering something you thought they'd forgotten.

little things.

the sort of things that don't look like much from far away, but somehow mean everything when they're yours.

and maybe that's what this little book was supposed to be.

not something that needed to be finished.

not something that needed to be read all at once.

just somewhere to leave a few thoughts.

one night at a time.

one chapter a night.

until eventually there were enough little chapters that the book felt like it had always been there.

so if you're reading this late at night, when everything feels quieter than usual, there's nowhere else you need to be right now.

you can just stay here for a while.

goodnight, hannah, i love you.

— Pedro ♡
